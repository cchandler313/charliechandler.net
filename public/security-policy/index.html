Responsible disclosure

Found something?

If you believe you have discovered a security issue affecting charliechandler.net, I appreciate responsible reports that help me understand and correct the problem without putting visitors or systems at unnecessary risk.

How to report an issue

Email charlie@charliechandler.net with a clear description of what you found, the affected URL or component, reproducible steps, and any supporting evidence that is safe to share.

Please include

  • The affected page, endpoint, or service.
  • Steps required to reproduce the behavior.
  • Observed impact and what an attacker could reasonably do.
  • Relevant request/response details with secrets and unrelated personal data removed.
  • Your preferred contact information if you want a response.

Please avoid

  • Accessing, modifying, deleting, or downloading data that does not belong to you.
  • Disrupting availability, flooding services, or performing denial-of-service testing.
  • Social engineering, credential attacks, phishing, or testing unrelated third-party services.
  • Publishing an unresolved vulnerability before there has been a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it.

What to expect

I will make a reasonable effort to acknowledge credible reports, reproduce the issue, and remediate confirmed vulnerabilities. This is a personal site, not a formal bug-bounty program, so monetary rewards are not promised.

Scope

About Charlie

Engineering reliable infrastructure with security built in.

I’m a network engineer with more than 16 years of experience supporting and evolving enterprise infrastructure. My work centers on availability, secure connectivity, operational clarity, and the engineering decisions that keep production environments dependable.

Professional focus

My background is rooted in enterprise networking: routing and switching, wireless, firewalls, VPN and remote access, troubleshooting, network architecture, and the day-to-day operational work required to keep critical services connected.

Security is inseparable from that work. Network boundaries, identity, segmentation, telemetry, access policy, and failure visibility all shape an organization’s security posture. I approach cybersecurity from that infrastructure perspective, with an emphasis on controls that are understandable, observable, and maintainable.

How I approach engineering

I prefer clear architecture over unnecessary complexity. Define trust boundaries, reduce exposure, document assumptions, make failures visible, automate repeatable work, and leave the environment easier to operate than it was before.

Where I’m expanding

I’m continuing to build deeper hands-on capability in defensive security, detection engineering, centralized logging, vulnerability management, identity-aware access, threat intelligence, security automation, and lab-driven incident analysis.

Beyond production networks

My lab work gives me room to test ideas end to end: segmented infrastructure, secure remote access, self-hosted services, Linux systems, virtualization, telemetry pipelines, automation, and security tooling. The goal is practical understanding, not checkbox familiarity.
